# Pre-specified criteria: verbatim excerpt and provenance

The study's two decision criteria were written into a design document in the authors' private
development repository before any interval method was run on test data. That document also
contains private planning notes, so it is not published in full. This file reproduces, verbatim
and in the original Chinese, every passage of it that defines or qualifies the criteria, with
English translations. The paper (Section 5, "Pre-specified criteria") refers to this file.

## Passage 1: decisions 19–21 (commit 15a90c7)

Original:

> **成功与失败标准**
> 19. 审计成立：标准共形预测下，至少一类天气的覆盖率比目标低 5 个百分点以上，且在多数电站上复现。
> 20. 方法成立：3 类天气覆盖率都在目标 ±2 个百分点以内；晴天区间比标准共形预测窄；分层覆盖率不输 CACP。
> 21. 失败预案：审计不成立时，转向“区间宽度与效率”的写法，或改做日内预测。

Translation:

> **Success and failure criteria**
> 19. The audit holds: under standard conformal prediction, coverage in at least one weather class is more than 5 percentage points below the target, and this is reproduced at a majority of stations.
> 20. The method holds: coverage in all 3 weather classes is within ±2 percentage points of the target; clear-day intervals are narrower than under standard conformal prediction; the stratified coverage is no worse than CACP's.
> 21. Fallback: if the audit does not hold, reframe the paper around interval width and efficiency, or switch to intraday forecasting.

In the paper, decision 19 is Criterion A and decision 20 is Criterion B (B1: within ±2 points;
B2: narrower clear-day intervals; B3: no worse than CACP). Decision 21 is a planning note, not a
criterion.

## Passage 2: Section 7 heading text (commit 15a90c7)

Original:

> 在实验开始前写定，见决策 19–21。全部以 α = 0.1（目标覆盖率 90%）的结果为准，80% 和 95% 只作为补充报告。

Translation:

> Fixed before the experiments start; see decisions 19–21. All criteria are judged on the
> α = 0.1 results (target coverage 90%); 80% and 95% are reported only as supplements.

## Passage 3: statistical uncertainty (added in commit 7698d8f)

Original:

> **统计不确定性（代码审查后补充）**：每站每类天气的测试天数只有约 15–50 天，分层覆盖率的按天块标准误约 1–2.5 个百分点，与 ±2 个百分点的判据同一量级。因此：
> - 分层覆盖率一律报告按天块自助法（day-block bootstrap）的 95% 置信区间；
> - 决策 19、20 以主分析各站汇总结果及其置信区间判定，不只看点估计。

Translation:

> **Statistical uncertainty (added after code review):** each station has only about 15–50 test
> days per weather class, so the day-block standard error of class coverage is about 1–2.5
> percentage points, the same order as the ±2-point criterion. Therefore:
> - class coverage is always reported with 95% day-block bootstrap confidence intervals;
> - decisions 19 and 20 are judged on the pooled results of the main-analysis stations together
>   with their confidence intervals, not on point estimates alone.

The passage does not say how point estimates and intervals are to be combined. The paper reports
Criterion A on station-level point estimates, with pooled intervals as context, and gives three
readings of Criterion B1 (Section 5 of the paper). The verdict for every reading is generated by
`python -m scripts.check_criteria` and recorded in `results/m3_m4_verdict.md`.
